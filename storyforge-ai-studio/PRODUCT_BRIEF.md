# Product brief

## Working product name
StoryForge AI Studio (placeholder; perform brand/domain/trademark checks before public launch).

## Vision
Make high-quality story-driven short-form video creation accessible to people who are good at explaining things but do not want to spend hours editing.

## Primary job to be done
"When I record myself explaining something, help me turn it into an engaging vertical video with relevant visuals and clean captions, without requiring me to become a video editor."

## Initial target users
1. Educators, tutors and trainers.
2. Coaches, consultants and professional explainers.
3. Founders and personal brands.

Start with educators and explainers because they naturally benefit from supporting visuals, examples, diagrams and key-point captions. Validate with real interviews before broadening the market.

## Core workflow
1. Create project; record in-app or import video(s).
2. Select output goal (Reel/Short/Story), language and editing intensity.
3. Upload original media privately; show clear upload progress.
4. Transcribe speech with timestamps and identify speech/pause regions.
5. Analyse story structure and identify visual-worthy concepts.
6. Propose cuts and filler-word handling with confidence/reason; user can accept, reject or adjust.
7. Produce two independent text layers: verbatim transcript/accessibility captions and concise editorial key-point captions.
8. Suggest supporting visuals, with asset origin, license, creator and attribution metadata. Offer image generation only if an approved local or paid provider is configured.
9. Let user select asset, choose picture-in-picture or cutaway, adjust timing/position/scale/crop, and preview entrance/exit/zoom motion.
10. Apply optional audio enhancement and basic colour/light correction.
11. Preview a low-resolution render; render final export in the background.
12. Export MP4 and save project for later edits.

## Key differentiator
**Story-aware visual suggestions with timestamped, editable placement.** The AI should understand the meaning and narrative of the recording, not merely decorate it with random B-roll.

## MVP scope
- One primary talking-head video, with a path to multiple clips.
- 9:16 vertical output at 1080x1920; optional 720p preview.
- Transcript with word/segment timestamps where available.
- Suggested removable pauses and filler-word cuts.
- Editorial captions grounded in transcript spans.
- Three or more relevant visual candidates per visual beat when the provider returns enough results.
- User-approved asset placement, PIP or cutaway.
- Subtle zoom/pan transitions and adjustable timing.
- Basic noise reduction, high-pass/EQ, loudness normalization and limiter presets.
- Basic exposure/contrast/saturation/white-balance controls; no promise of physically accurate AI relighting in MVP.
- Non-destructive edit manifest, undo/redo and project autosave.
- Background rendering, progress, retry and export download/share sheet.
- Privacy controls, deletion and user-visible media retention.

## Not MVP
- Full nonlinear multitrack editor.
- AI-generated video B-roll by default.
- Automatic Instagram publishing.
- Automatic face/eye retouching or identity modification.
- Music catalogue or licensed sound library.
- Team collaboration, agency workspaces, analytics or scheduling.
- “Magic sun” relighting that claims physically realistic illumination; revisit only after quality research.

## Product principles
- User control over automation.
- Non-destructive editing and original preservation.
- Evidence-grounded captions and suggestions.
- Low-latency preview; background final render.
- Great defaults, adjustable details.
- Private media by default.
- Transparent cost and usage limits.
- No fake AI result when a provider is unavailable.

## Success metrics for private beta
These are proposed experiment thresholds, not industry benchmarks:
- At least 6 of 10 pilot creators return and create another project in week two.
- At least 70% of proposed cut decisions are accepted or lightly adjusted.
- At least 50% of visual suggestions are accepted, replaced or repositioned rather than ignored.
- Median user-reported editing time is materially lower than their previous workflow.
- At least 3 of 10 pilot users express willingness to pay after using a real export.
- Track render success rate, time-to-first-preview, cost per exported minute, crash-free sessions and deletion completion.

## Positioning draft
"Record a video. Let AI understand your story. Get a polished Reel." Avoid claims like “professional quality guaranteed” until supported by measured results.
