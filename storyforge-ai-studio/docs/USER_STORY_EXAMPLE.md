# Example story-to-edit behavior

## Input speech
"Yesterday, I called a plumber because the tap in my kitchen was leaking. He came, opened the tap, checked the pipe, and found that the washer was damaged."

## Expected structured output (illustrative only)
- Story beat A: kitchen tap leak; transcript span linked; suggest leaking faucet photo.
- Story beat B: plumber opens tap; suggest plumber repairing faucet; place at exact phrase timestamp.
- Story beat C: pipe inspection; suggest under-sink pipe inspection image.
- Story beat D: damaged washer; suggest close-up plumbing washer/gasket.

## Editorial captions
- "A leaking kitchen tap"
- "The plumber inspected the pipe"
- "The washer was damaged"

Each caption links to transcript segment IDs and is editable. Do not fabricate the cause, price, brand, location or repair advice if the speaker did not say it.

## Composition
Default to speaker full frame with small PIP only if it remains legible. Offer full-frame cutaway with original speaker audio continuing. Zoom should be subtle, user-controllable and avoid motion sickness.
