# Quality steering

- “Done” means code implemented, tests actually executed, acceptance criteria checked and docs updated.
- No unsupported claim that an AI model supports a language or produces a quality level; evaluate real samples.
- Preserve source truth in transcription and editorial captions; cite transcript segment IDs.
- Render tests must inspect output with ffprobe and verify A/V stream, duration, dimensions, orientation and audio presence.
- UI work includes loading, empty, error, offline, permissions and low-storage states.
- Verify Android/iOS on their real toolchains. Do not claim iOS verified from Windows/Linux.
- Avoid visual clutter. Prioritize preview, story beats, clear approval actions and safe-zone-aware captions.
